Verifier convention:
  Power iteration only finds a nonnegative witness vector.  Acceptance is the
  subsequent statewise Collatz check.  Positive binary64 inputs are summed in
  long double and every source sum is enlarged by 1e-10; the target side is
  reduced by the same relative envelope.  For the tilted run, the 27 values
  exp((3/20)d), -13 <= d <= 13, are replaced by hard-coded decimal rational
  upper bounds obtained from Taylor intervals.  Zero target states are
  accepted only when the uninflated source sum is exactly zero.
